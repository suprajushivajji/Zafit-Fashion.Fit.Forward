# ZAFIT - Fashion.Fit.Forward

ZAFIT is a modern ecommerce fashion brand established in 2025, dedicated to bringing cutting-edge style and quality to fashion enthusiasts worldwide. Our platform curates the finest trend collections from top designers and renowned labels, all in one convenient place.

## About ZAFIT
At ZAFIT, we believe style is more than just clothing—it's a statement of who you are and who you aspire to be. Founded with a passion for fashion, ZAFIT curates the finest collections from top fashion houses around the globe.

Our mission is simple: to elevate your style through thoughtfully selected pieces that blend contemporary aesthetics with timeless appeal. Each item in our collection is chosen not just for its visual impact, but for its quality craftsmanship and innovative design.

When you shop with ZAFIT, you're not just buying clothing—you're investing in pieces that tell your unique story. Join us on this journey as we redefine what it means to dress with intention and purpose.

## Features
- Modern, responsive ecommerce website
- Curated collections from top fashion brands
- Secure shopping and payment options
- User-friendly navigation and engaging UI
- Brand and product showcase

## Getting Started
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser to explore the website.
3. All assets (images, videos, styles, and scripts) are included in the project directory.

## Project Structure
- `index.html` - Main landing page
- `about.html` - About the brand
- `style.css` - Stylesheet for layout and design
- `script.js` - JavaScript for interactivity
- `images/` - Image assets
- `videos/` - Video assets
